The evaluation matrix

Every model.
Every benchmark.
One table.

Sixty-one releases from eleven labs, from the November 2022 demo to this month's frontier, set against twenty-one published evaluations. Models run across the top in release order, evaluations down the side. The table opens on the present — scroll left to travel back to 2022. The first column and the header row stay put, and the best published figure on each benchmark is highlighted.

Most of this table is empty, and that is the finding. Labs publish the benchmarks they win. A blank cell means no published figure, never a zero — and never a number we inferred, interpolated or estimated to fill a gap. The two evaluations with the broadest independent coverage, GDPval and METR, are also the two hardest to game, which is why the index leans on them.

Figures are quoted as published by their maintainers, the releasing lab, or independent evaluators, at the dates shown. Where a lab published a range or revised a score after launch, the note on the model's own page says so. Every model name links to its page. † marks an extrapolation rather than a measurement.
