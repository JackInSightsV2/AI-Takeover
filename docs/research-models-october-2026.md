# October 2026 model research

Checked 11 October 2026. This is a broad primary-source refresh, not a provably exhaustive census of every checkpoint or fine-tune worldwide. The starting roster contained 61 models across 11 labs and 145 populated benchmark cells out of 1,464 (9.9%).

Added 67 individually named releases or variants, including specialist image, audio, embedding and translation models. Restricted-access releases are labelled. Product launches, speed tiers of an existing model and announcements without usable access are not automatically new models. New entries have no inferred displacement position.

The table gives the displayed release month. Decimal-year values are ordering aids, not exact release-date records. In particular the official Fable/Mythos article specifies September, and the Flash-Next card specifies August. GLM-5.3 official notes date their entry 18 August while contemporaneous initial-access reports say 14 August. OCR 4.1 launched in July and became GA in August.

## Coverage and limits

All eleven existing labs were searched: Alibaba, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, MiniMax, Mistral, Moonshot, OpenAI, Z.ai and xAI. Cohere and Microsoft added. Moonshot's [official help overview](https://www.kimi.ai/help/agent/agent-overview) still names July K3; no later released variant was verified. [MiniMax's index](https://www.minimax.io/blog) yielded Music 3.0; [Mistral's changelog](https://docs.mistral.ai/resources/changelogs) distinguished OCR GA from its earlier release. AI21 was swept without a newly verified model.

This is not all of AI: model hubs contain many fine-tunes, quantizations and specialist releases beyond these labs. StepFun, Kling, Vidu and additional scientific models surfaced during the sweep but their original launch/variant details were not fully verified, so they are remaining coverage gaps, not claims of absence. GLM FlashX is a documented faster hosted variant; equivalence to Flash is not established and its separate launch details remain a coverage gap. Google Live Avatar adds a feature to Live rather than a separate confirmed model.

JEV ask returned SKIP/unavailable and check returned UNAVAILABLE, so it supplied no validation. Agent-Reach/Exa CLI was absent; the web tool was used to open first-party release pages, documentation and model cards. No paid inference or benchmark reproduction was performed.

Final audit: 128 models, 24 evaluations, 13 labs; 154 / 3,072 cells (5.0%). Nine verified cells added; coverage percentage falls because specialist/new-model blanks remain honest. Node syntax check passed. JEV remained unavailable. Official Hugging Face cards additionally verify open weights for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3 Flash. Unverified weights status is omitted for hosted specialist entries rather than asserted closed.
